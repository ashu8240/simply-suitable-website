What KYC is, and how it works for mutual funds

12 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

KYC stands for Know Your Customer. It is a one-time check of who you are, completed before you can invest in mutual funds. It is required by law, to help prevent fraud and money laundering.

The short version

  • KYC is a one-time identity and address verification that comes before your first mutual fund investment.
  • You usually need your PAN, proof of address, a photo and your bank details. Many people can complete it online.
  • Your KYC record is kept by registered KYC agencies, so you usually do not need to repeat it for each fund house.

What KYC involves

  1. You give your details: name, PAN, date of birth, address, mobile number and email.
  2. Your identity and address are verified, either online, for example using an Aadhaar-based process, or with documents in person.
  3. A photo, and sometimes a video check, is taken, depending on how you apply.
  4. Your record is stored with a registered KYC agency.
  5. Your status is updated, and once it shows as validated you can invest.

The exact steps and documents depend on how you apply and on current rules, so confirm them with the platform or intermediary you use.

What else you need to start investing

  • A bank account in your own name.
  • A choice of nominee for your investments.
  • A declaration of your tax residency.

These are usually collected on a short account-opening form after KYC is complete.

Common reasons for delays

  • Your name or address does not match across your PAN and your address proof.
  • The documents or photos are unclear.
  • Your mobile number or email is out of date.
  • Your KYC status shows as “on hold” until a missing detail is corrected.

Staying safe

  • Never share an OTP or a password with anyone.
  • Pay only to the fund house or scheme accounts shown on the official form or platform, never to an individual’s personal account.
  • Check that the person helping you is registered. A distributor’s ARN can be checked.
  • Keep your mobile number and email updated, because statements and alerts come to them.

With me, your money never passes through me. Payments go directly to the scheme or fund house.

Words to know

KYC
Know Your Customer: the one-time identity and address check required before you invest.
KRA
KYC Registration Agency: an agency that stores KYC records so they can be used across intermediaries.
PAN
Permanent Account Number: the tax identity needed for investing.
Nominee
The person you name to receive your investments if something happens to you.
ARN
AMFI Registration Number: the registration number of a mutual fund distributor.

Common questions

Do I need to do KYC again for each fund house?

Usually not. A completed KYC is generally accepted across fund houses, but a fund house may ask for some extra details when you open your account.

How long does KYC last?

It is generally a one-time process, though you may be asked to update your details if they change.

Can I check my KYC status?

Usually yes. You can check it online through your platform or the agency that holds your record.
