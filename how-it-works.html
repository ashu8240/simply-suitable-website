How it works

The Simply Suitable 5-Step Financial Framework: Understand. Organise. Plan. Implement. Review.

  1. Understand

    Your current financial situation in plain terms: income, spending, savings, loans, and what worries you.

  2. Organise

    Bring your financial information into one clear picture, so nothing is scattered across apps, folders and old emails.

  3. Plan

    Turn your goals into a strategy, such as a home, your child’s education or retirement, with the right approach for each.

  4. Implement

    Put suitable mutual fund solutions in place for the plan you agreed on.

  5. Review

    Check progress regularly and adjust when your life changes.

Your first call

It lasts 25 to 30 minutes and needs no preparation. We talk about where you are and what you want, and you leave with a clear idea of the next step, whether or not we end up working together.

Questions people ask

What is a Mutual Fund Distributor?

An AMFI-registered intermediary who helps you choose and invest in mutual fund schemes. My ARN is 356057.

Do you guarantee returns?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, and nobody can promise returns.

What should I have ready?

Only a rough idea of your income, expenses, loans and any existing investments. The checklist on the Resources page helps.

I already invest. Is this still useful?

Yes. Many people start by reviewing what they hold and whether it still fits their goals.
