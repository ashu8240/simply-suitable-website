How it works

Understand. Organise. Plan. Implement. Review.

You do not need to have everything figured out before we speak. This is how a first conversation becomes a plan that fits your circumstances and priorities.

  1. Understand

    Your current financial situation: where you stand today and what prompted you to reach out.

  2. Organise

    Your financial information, brought into one clear picture, only where it is useful.

  3. Plan

    Your goals and the right strategy, separating immediate priorities from longer-term objectives.

  4. Implement

    Suitable solutions, once you are comfortable with the approach.

  5. Review

    Your progress, and any changes in your circumstances.

What you can expect

You do not need to have everything figured out before we speak.

  • A personalised understanding of your financial position
  • Identification and prioritisation of your key financial goals
  • Review of existing investments and cash-flow requirements
  • Assessment of relevant financial protection gaps
  • Goal-based investment planning where suitable
  • Ongoing guidance and periodic review
  • A practical and easy-to-understand approach

Before our first conversation

There is nothing to fill in beforehand and nothing to sign. There is no obligation to proceed afterwards. The paperwork and practical details can wait until we both know whether working together makes sense.

Whenever you’re ready, we can schedule a time to speak. There is no pressure to make a decision during our first conversation.

Questions people ask

What are you registered as?

I’m an AMFI-Registered Mutual Fund Distributor, ARN-356057, operating within the applicable regulatory framework of SEBI and AMFI.

Do you guarantee returns?

No. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks, and no return, outcome or tax benefit is guaranteed.

Do I transfer money to you?

No. Money never passes through me. Investment and premium payments go through the authorised provider, and I will never ask you to send money to a personal account.

What should I have ready?

Nothing for our first conversation. If we go ahead, I’ll ask for statements and details only where they are useful.

I already invest. Is this still useful?

Yes. Reviewing your existing investments and how they connect to your goals is part of the process.
